Potato Partnerships

Data centres, hardware operators, software platforms, and enterprise teams can use Potato to expose capacity through one marketplace with clear economics and operational controls.

Data centres

List spare regional capacity, improve utilisation, and offer buyers a flexible alternative for burst workloads. Potato can support bulk listing workflows and negotiated rates for large pools of machines.

Enterprise fleets

Bring approved internal or partner-owned nodes into a managed fleet. Fleet operators receive visibility into availability, benchmark performance, queue demand, and SLA recovery.

API integrations

Use programmatic discovery and priority rental workflows to connect Potato with internal schedulers, developer portals, research platforms, or capacity planning tools.

A practical partnership model

Partnerships begin with a capacity and security review, followed by a listing plan that defines hardware metadata, pricing, regions, support contacts, and escalation expectations. Larger partners can discuss bulk listing rates, onboarding assistance, and API integration support.

We aim for partnerships that are measurable and durable: better utilisation for providers, predictable access for buyers, and transparent records for everyone involved.
