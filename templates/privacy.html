Privacy Policy

Information we collect

We collect account details, company information supplied for enterprise review, listing specifications, agreement and escrow records, ratings, and operational logs needed to provide the marketplace.

How we use information

Information is used to authenticate users, match buyers and sellers, calculate fees, administer escrow and SLA failover, review enterprise applications, prevent abuse, and support disputes.

Security and retention

Access is limited to the participating parties and authorized administrators. Do not upload passwords, API keys, private files, or sensitive personal data. Records are retained only as long as needed for service delivery, dispute handling, and legal obligations.
