Careers at Potato

Potato is building a more open, efficient, and human-scale way to access compute. We are looking for people who enjoy difficult systems problems and care about the trust required to run infrastructure on a global marketplace.

Our team values practical experimentation, careful security thinking, direct communication, and a willingness to improve the product from real operator and customer feedback. We support focused work, thoughtful documentation, and people who can explain complex ideas clearly.

Open roles

Senior Distributed Systems Engineer

Design scheduling, queueing, failover, and observability systems for a marketplace of heterogeneous CPU and GPU nodes. You will work across APIs, persistence, and operational tooling.

What you bring: Production Python or Go, distributed systems fundamentals, and experience debugging stateful services.

Trust & Safety Analyst

Help define and enforce safe compute usage policies. Investigate suspicious workload patterns, improve provider onboarding, and partner with engineering on practical abuse-prevention controls.

What you bring: Strong analytical judgment, careful written communication, and interest in cybersecurity or online platforms.

Enterprise Solutions Engineer

Work with organisations operating private fleets and large workloads to design integrations, capacity plans, and API adoption strategies that fit their teams.

What you bring: Customer empathy, technical fluency, and experience translating business requirements into reliable systems.

Frontend Product Engineer

Build calm, precise interfaces for sellers, buyers, and fleet operators. Turn dense operational data into workflows that are easy to scan and hard to misuse.

What you bring: Modern JavaScript, accessible HTML/CSS, and a strong product sense for dashboards and tools.

How we work

We prefer small, well-understood changes, honest status updates, and decisions that can be revisited when new evidence arrives. Potato is an equal opportunity workplace, and we welcome applicants with different paths into technology.
