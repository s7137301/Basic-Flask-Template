Potato Help Center

Find clear answers about compute listings, rental queues, priority jobs, escrow, failover, and account security.

Choose a listing, select the number of hours, review the seller payout and platform fee, then confirm the agreement. The buyer payment is recorded as held in escrow while the contract is active.

You can join the standard queue, or choose Priority Compute when available. Priority jobs are activated immediately and use the premium pricing shown at checkout.

When failover is available and a node disconnects, completed hours are accounted for and remaining work may move to an eligible backup node. The agreement records the offload and any platform penalty.

Use accurate CPU and GPU names, provide a realistic hourly rate, keep benchmark information current, and maintain the node during booked hours.
