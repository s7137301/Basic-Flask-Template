SLA failover guarantee

When a node disconnects, eligible remaining hours can be offloaded at 80% of the base rate. The original seller is paid for completed hours and a 20% platform penalty is retained for the disrupted portion.

Frequently asked questions

How are sellers paid?

Sellers receive 100% of their listed hourly price for completed workload hours. The buyer pays the platform fee on top.

What does enterprise approval change?

Approved enterprise buyers receive enterprise contract terms and a 22% platform fee. Applications are reviewed by an administrator.

When does failover apply?

Failover applies only when enabled at checkout, the agreement is valid, and an available secondary listing can accept the remaining hours.
