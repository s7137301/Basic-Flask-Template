Master Platform Compute Agreement

This agreement defines the binding rules between Buyers, Sellers, and the Platform.

1. Parties and service

The Seller supplies the listed compute capacity, the Buyer purchases authorized workload time, and the Platform provides discovery, contract records, escrow accounting, and administrative controls.

2. Pricing and escrow

The Seller's payout equals the listed hourly price multiplied by completed hours. The Buyer pays that payout plus the applicable platform fee: 15% for standard buyers and 22% for approved enterprise buyers. At confirmation, the payment is recorded as held in escrow.

3. Availability and failover

Automatic SLA Workload Failover applies only when selected at checkout. After a node interruption, completed hours are paid pro rata to the original Seller. Remaining hours may be assigned to an available secondary Seller at 80% of the original base rate. A 20% platform penalty is retained for the disrupted portion and the agreement escrow state becomes partial_released.

4. Acceptable use and security

Parties must use the service lawfully, protect passwords and credentials, and avoid malware, abuse, unauthorized access, denial-of-service activity, and workloads that damage or interfere with infrastructure. Profile uploads and workload inputs must not contain secrets or unsafe content.

5. Records and disputes

Agreement, rating, escrow, and SLA records may be retained for service delivery, support, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. The Platform may suspend accounts or listings that breach this agreement.
